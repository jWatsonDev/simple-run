Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Ruck & Run is designed with privacy in mind. Everything the app records or reads stays on your iPhone. We operate no servers, require no account, and never receive, sell, or share your data.

Location Data

Ruck & Run uses your iPhone's GPS while you are actively tracking a run, walk, or ruck to measure distance and pace and to draw your route. Location continues while your screen is locked so tracking isn't interrupted. Location is only used during an activity you start, is stored only on your device, and is never sent to us or any third party.

Motion Data

The app uses your iPhone's barometer to measure elevation gain during an activity. This reading stays on your device.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Ruck & Run connects to Apple Health to rate how hard each activity was and explain why.

Data the app reads from Apple Health:

  • Heart rate during your activity, resting heart rate, and heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Sleep
  • VO2 max, body weight, and date of birth (to estimate your heart-rate range)
  • Number of alcoholic beverages, if you log them
  • Past walking, running, and hiking workouts and their routes (to learn your personal baseline)

Data the app saves to Apple Health: your completed runs, walks, and rucks as workouts, including distance, route, and estimated active calories.

Health data is used only inside the app to show you your own results. It is processed on your device, never leaves your device through Ruck & Run, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared with or sold to anyone. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Ruck & Run, or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Activity History and Notes

Your activity history (distance, time, pace, route, elevation, heart-rate readings, difficulty scores) and any optional notes you add about sleep, drinks, or food are stored locally on your device. We have no access to this data. Deleting the app deletes this history; workouts already saved to Apple Health remain in Apple Health under your control.

Voice Updates and Lock Screen

Spoken split updates are generated on your device. Lock Screen and Dynamic Island updates are created by the app on your device; no push notification service is used.

Sharing

Share images are created on your device and only leave it when you choose to share them using the iOS share sheet.

Maps

Maps are provided by Apple Maps. To display a map, your device loads map imagery from Apple, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Ruck & Run does not send your activity data to Apple Maps.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account or login
  • No analytics or crash reporting
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No data shared with or sold to third parties

Children's Privacy

Ruck & Run does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
