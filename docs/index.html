Ruck & Run

A simple run, walk, and ruck tracker that tells you how hard each session really was — and why.

Getting Started

Pick Run, Walk, or Ruck (set your ruck weight), then tap Start. Allow location access and Apple Health when asked. Tap Finish when you're done and confirm End & Save. You'll get a difficulty score from 1 to 10, the reasons behind it, and charts for your splits, elevation, and heart rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the difficulty score calculated?

It combines your pace, hills, ruck weight, and duration. When heart rate is available from your Apple Watch, the score is based on how hard your body actually worked, compared with your own normal from past workouts. The explanation also looks at last night's sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, and any notes you add.

How do I get heart rate?

Wear your Apple Watch and start an Outdoor Run or Walk in the Watch's Workout app along with Ruck & Run. The app reads the heart rate from Apple Health when you finish. Without a workout running on the Watch, heart rate is only sampled every few minutes and the score is estimated from pace, hills, and weight.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. With your permission it reads heart rate, sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, VO2 max, body weight, and past workouts, and saves your activities as workouts with route and estimated active calories, so they count toward your Move ring. Manage access in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Ruck & Run.

How do voice updates work?

By default the app announces your distance, time, and average pace every half mile, lowering your music while it speaks. Change the interval or turn it off with Voice updates on the main screen.

Can I see my run on the Lock Screen?

Yes. Time, distance, and pace appear on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while you're tracking. If you don't see them, go to Settings → Ruck & Run and turn on Live Activities.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. Ruck & Run has no servers and no account. Deleting the app deletes your history, except workouts already saved to Apple Health. See the Privacy Policy.

The map isn't showing my location. What do I do?

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Ruck & Run and choose "While Using the App".

Why does the app keep tracking when my screen is locked?

GPS continues in the background during an activity you started, so your distance and route stay accurate while your phone is in your pocket. Tracking stops as soon as you end the activity.
